Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

PostReady is a hackathon demo that turns a product link into a short video. This page says what it handles when you use the live generator.

What we receive when you sign in

Sign-in uses Google. We ask Google only for your basic identity: your Google account ID and email address. We do not get access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your account.

What we keep

  • A counter per Google account ID, so each account can make a limited number of videos. It stores the account ID, a number, and the list of videos that account has made, so they can be shown back to you under My videos. Your email address is shown back to you on the page but is not stored with the counter.
  • The videos you generate, with the product page data and photos they were made from, on the machine that renders them. They are reachable only through the unguessable link shown to you.

Your sign-in token is held in your browser for the current tab session and is cleared when you sign out or close the tab.

Who else sees data

  • Cerebras runs the Kimi K3.1 Pro model. It receives the product page text and product photos for the link you submit. It does not receive your name, email or account ID.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site and the counter.
  • The store whose link you submit is contacted to read its public product page.

We do not sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

Removal and questions

To have your counter or a generated video removed, or to ask anything else, open an issue at github.com/backspacevenkat/kimi-product-shorts.
